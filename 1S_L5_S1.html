① [ ]内の語を並べかえましょう。　② 下線部を変えて対話をしましょう。

I [ in / enjoy / running ] the park.

→ I enjoy running in the park.

A: What do you do for your health?
B: I enjoy running in the park. How about you?
A: I enjoy getting up early in the morning.
B: That's nice!
