① [ ]内の語を並べかえましょう。　② 下線部を変えて対話をしましょう。

I like dogs [ than / cats / better ].

→ I like dogs better than cats.

A: Which do you like better, dogs or cats?
B: I like dogs better than cats.
A: Oh, me too!
B: They are really cute.
