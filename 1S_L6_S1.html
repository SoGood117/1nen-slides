① [ ]内の語を並べかえましょう。　② 下線部を変えて対話をしましょう。

Because math is [ as / as / interesting ] solving a puzzle.

→ Because math is as interesting as solving a puzzle.

A: What subjects do you like?
B: I like math.
A: Why?
B: Because it is as interesting as solving a puzzle.
